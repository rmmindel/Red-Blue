# Working together

- `main` is the shared, working version. Nobody commits to it directly.
- Each person works on their own branch (`fricke`, `mindel`).
- To share work: push your branch, open a pull request into `main`, and have the other person review it.
- Before starting new work, update your branch: `git checkout <your-branch> && git pull origin main`.
- Keep commits small and messages short and clear.

See [docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md) for what we are building and who owns what.
